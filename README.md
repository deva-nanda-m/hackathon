## 🏆 Hackathon Project

**After the Collapse** was developed as part of a **group hackathon organized by TinkerHub VJEC**.

The project was built collaboratively under hackathon time constraints, with a focus on turning a disaster scenario into an interactive engineering challenge.

The team focused on combining:

* 🎮 Game design
* ⚙️ Custom physics simulation
* 🏗️ Structural engineering concepts
* 💡 Problem solving
* 🎨 Interactive UI/UX
* 🌍 Disaster and rescue scenarios

Rather than building a full-scale physics engine, the project uses a lightweight custom structural simulation designed specifically around the game's requirements.

The hackathon format encouraged us to prioritize:

> **A working, playable, and engaging experience over unnecessary technical complexity.**

## 🎯 Hackathon Objective

The core idea was to explore how **physics-based problem solving can be transformed into an engaging browser game**.

Players aren't simply asked to construct a bridge. As the missions progress, they encounter different engineering problems involving:

```text
Construction
     ↓
Resource Management
     ↓
Physics
     ↓
Environmental Hazards
     ↓
Structural Failure
     ↓
Problem Solving
```

The result is a game where **failure is part of the gameplay**: players observe what went wrong, modify their design, and try again.
